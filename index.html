The problem

A ringing phone you can’t reach is a customer walking to your competitor

Reviews matter once a month. The phone rings every day — usually at the exact moment you’re busiest. For a typical small restaurant or shop, the pattern looks like this:

~150

calls a month go unanswered during rush hours, breaks, and after close

~60%

of those missed calls are real buying intent — an order, a booking, or a catering quote

69%

of callers who reach no one don’t leave a message — they call the next place on the list

Illustrative figures for a busy single-location shop, consistent with widely reported small-business phone studies. Your first week of call logs gives us your real numbers — that’s where we start.

The number is almost never lost

When caller ID is available it’s captured on every call — even if the caller hangs up mid-conversation. If a number is withheld or blocked, the AI asks the caller to leave a contact number for your callback, and that number goes on the list instead.

📝

You call back informed

ChatGPT’s summary tells you the size of the opportunity before you dial. A $700 catering lead gets your first call — not whoever happened to ring last.

⏱️

Urgency is sorted for you

Call back today = money or a promise with a deadline. Handled by AI = answered completely, listed only so you can see the full day. Nothing hides, nothing nags.

🤝

The AI never closes a big deal alone

Quotes, catering, and complaints are always handed to you. The AI’s job is to make sure the opportunity survives until you can take it — not to replace your judgment.

What the AI handles — and what stays with you

✅ The AI handles it fully

  • Hours, location, parking, menu basics
  • Takeout orders with pickup time and name
  • Small reservations (under your size limit)
  • “Do you have…?” availability questions
  • Repeating the same answers, in 3 languages, at 9 PM or 9 AM

🙋 Always handed to you

  • Catering quotes and large group bookings
  • Complaints — the AI apologizes, takes details, and flags it urgent
  • Anything about price negotiation or discounts
  • Allergy or health questions beyond your written info sheet
  • Any caller who asks for a person — immediately, no argument

How we’d start

Trust first, phone second — a two-step rollout

Handing your phone to an AI is the highest-trust step a shop takes. So we don’t start there. We earn it with something smaller, then switch the phone on together.

Step 1 · First session

Reviews & everyday inquiries, in ChatGPT

We start with one repetitive task that takes your time every week — replying to customer reviews and inquiries — and build a simple ChatGPT workflow for it together, using your shop’s own real examples. You also get your shop’s phone info sheet written here: hours, menu, catering rules — the exact facts the AI will later speak from.

  • Runs on the shop’s own ChatGPT — no new system to learn
  • You practice until you and your staff can run it without help

Step 2 · Once step 1 feels easy

AI phone answering, switched on together

We connect the AI to your number for the hours you choose — lunch rush first, then evenings. Every call is summarized by ChatGPT into your callback list, so a catering or group-order inquiry is never lost. You review the first week’s summaries with support on hand, and adjust what the AI may promise.

  • Starts with rush hours only — your phone, your schedule
  • You can pause or switch it off at any time

Honest answers

Questions owners ask first

Will callers know it’s an AI?

Yes. The AI introduces itself as the shop’s phone assistant. In practice, callers care most about getting an answer and a promised callback that actually happens — and the callback list is what makes that promise reliable.

What if the AI gets something wrong?

It only speaks from the info sheet we write together in Step 1, and it never quotes prices for catering or large orders — those always become a callback for you. Every summary shows exactly what was said, so you can spot and fix a mistake the same day.

What does it cost me to try?

The first session is about your reviews-and-inquiries workflow in ChatGPT — no phone change at all. Phone answering is only switched on after that, for the hours you pick, and can be paused anytime. Exact service pricing depends on the phone agent provider and call volume, and we’d review real options together before anything is connected — nothing is signed up on your behalf.

My callers speak Spanish / Korean. Does it still work?

Yes — the AI detects the caller’s language and answers in it, while your summary and callback list always arrive in your preferred language. The Spanish scenario in the demo above shows exactly that flow.

Why ChatGPT, if a phone service takes the call?

Two parts work together: the phone agent holds the conversation, and ChatGPT does the office work — turning each call into a summary, a callback list, and your end-of-day digest, in the same tool you’ll already use for reviews and inquiries. One workflow to learn, not three.
